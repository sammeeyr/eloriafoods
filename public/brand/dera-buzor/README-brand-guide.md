# Dera Buzor Lounge — Brand Guide

Tagline: Taste More. Relax Better.

## Official colour palette
- Forest Green: #07552B — primary identity, headings and footer
- Vibrant Orange: #F28C00 — calls to action and promotions
- Fresh Green: #58A846 — freshness and availability accents
- Warm Cream: #FFF6D9 — primary background
- Pure White: #FFFFFF — cards and content surfaces
- Charcoal: #202820 — body text and dark surfaces

## Typography
- Wordmark and headings: Montserrat Bold / ExtraBold
- Supporting text: Poppins Regular / Medium
